01About

I'm a junior at Bellevue High School with a growing focus on biology, data, and the place where the two meet. Since 2025 I've been part of a research team at ASDRP studying precision medicine — how a person's genetic data can be used to shape treatment that actually fits them, rather than a one-size-fits-all protocol.

That interest carried into a second project with PathIvy, where I analyze gene expression data from cancer and normal cells to find where gene activity diverges, and use bioinformatics tools to investigate the genes behind it. Outside of research, I play flute in the marching band, compete in varsity doubles badminton, help run orientation for incoming students through Link Crew, and volunteer at a local Hopelink food bank.
